## Purpose

Give FlightDeck its manuals and its library, and a librarian who presides over them (VISION "Manuals and libraries": "Operator, technical and maintenance manuals created adjacent to the work and stored globally. Library for concepts, knowledge and best practices. kept fresh and updated. mid tier agents preside over the corpus, reviewing work against it, delivering it where it needs to go.").

After this pull request the owner can: read an operator manual that starts with the first-hour guide and explains the workshop lifecycle and the chain of command; read technical pages on how the parts work (including how a workshop's own notes and checks reach its agents) and a register of Claude Code facts with their verdict and source; read a maintenance page on how to change and test an agent, hook, skill or workflow, how to add an agent without adding a rank, and the correction ladder; read five library pages carried over from earlier branches in edited form plus a page of terms in plain words; ask the librarian (`flight:librarian`, Sonnet) a question and get an answer that cites `path:line`; type `/flight:manual <workshop>` to have a manual page drafted from a workshop's files and filed on the right shelf; type `/flight:library-check` to list stale pages and have the librarian re-check them; and run `flightdeck/workflows/library-review.js` after any piece of work to have the librarian check it against the corpus.

PR 7 depends on PR 1 only. It imports nothing from PRs 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 and its pages describe only what exists on its branch (PR 1 plus PR 7): "current form only".
