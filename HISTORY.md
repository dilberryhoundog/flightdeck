# Earlier work

Earlier attempts at FlightDeck are history on these branches. They hold manuals, library documents, agent definitions and systems, and they stay there as minable content.

- `archive/main-2026-10-09`: `main` as it stood before 2026-10-09
- `cockpit`: a pilot agent dispatching teams, its archived first version, and the concept notes
- `flightcrew-buildout-v1`, `flightcrew-buildout`, `flightcrew-core`: the flightcrew orchestration system
- `flightcrew-characterization`, `flightcrew-characterization-1/contracts`, `run/flightcrew-characterization-2`: the characterization test suite for flightcrew
- `engage-crew`, `rubric-testing`, `constitution-research`: agent specs, rubric experiments and principles documents

`dev/workspace/research/repo-reset/` indexes that work:

- `interview-record.md`: the owner's decisions of 2026-10-09, their starting list of parts, and the questions still open
- `user-intent.md`: what the owner has said across past sessions, in their own dated words
- `concept-trace.md`: the successive concept documents compared
- `work-inventory.md`: each body of earlier work and how far it got
- `branch-report.md`: where commits live across the branches
