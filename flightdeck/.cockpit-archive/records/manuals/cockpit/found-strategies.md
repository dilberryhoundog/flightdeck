# Found strategies for team performance

Practices the cockpit's flights have confirmed, collected for long-term reference as the commander asked. Each is one line: what to do, and why it holds.

## Attack

Live adversary from the first landing, reporting to the lead only; the lead relays once per round. Tandem adversaries when evidence lands faster than one attacks, split by object and declared at dispatch. A cold reader on the finished product, spawned after the freeze, checked by the live seats. The adversary attacks the dossier before it goes to the commander. A frozen pass is numbered, and frozen only when the writer acknowledges; every write is a new number.

## Brief

One closed brief per pass: which rounds it absorbs and that nothing further is coming. The lead's address stated at dispatch. Read scope as paths; one write path; a line cap; the commander's latest order on the subject in every seat's scope, verbatim. The owner's position is tested and filled out, not priced as one option among the seat's own. A mechanism's scope is read at its page, never inferred from its name. A seat's measurement is bounded by what the seat can see; ask who else could have changed something.

## Queue

A pooled task queue with dependencies and self-claim, the task subject naming which seat kind may take it; claim, then read the owner back. Claim and completion events emitted into the dispatch record, since the list keeps nothing. A seat told to list the folder and take the next unattacked item when a round ends.

## Type signalling

A real agent type on every seat, used at dispatch and relied on for which tasks a seat claims; audited afterwards from the seats' metadata.

## Reading the crew

A seat's state from its transcript on disk, never from its idle notice. A seat's system prompt from the transcript's prompt snapshot. A count from the filesystem with its pattern stated, never from a typed list. Relay a file's current figure, never a seat's earlier summary. Quote a finding and its id; do not forward a file.

## Testing

A fresh cheap agent given only the guidance, read from its transcript, is the cheapest proof the guidance is enough. Measure a harness claim by a headless launch reading output fields before a seat relies on it.

## Recording

A seat catching an error is written into the dispatch outcome as it happens, or it is lost. A withdrawal carries a reason. Every seat that flies has a dossier before the team closes.
