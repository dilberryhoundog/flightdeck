# XX-004 — A part approval hides which parts, and names them

Both sides come out of `cockpit.constitution` lines 44 and 71, which describe the same part approval from the two ends.

## CA-013

"However if dissatisfied, instead of digging into the details, the commander can 'part' approve. Which parts are accepted or not cannot be determined. Instead the disapproval is expressed by adding to, or adjusting the commanders own record." [44]

## CA-015

"These units come with preset flags (+ or - for markdown, yes or no for json) that enable the commander to advise the pilot which units make up the decision. The pilot starts with all their units as positive, the commander negates units (wich stay frozen, so redeciding is not repeated)." [71]

## The contradiction

CA-013 makes a part approval deliberately silent: the pilot is told some of the draft is unsatisfactory but not which, so the pilot must re-read the commander's own records and remake the draft against them rather than haggle over details. CA-015 gives the commander a flag on every unit of a decision and has them negate the units they do not want, which states exactly which parts are not accepted and freezes them so the pilot does not decide them again. The second mechanism does the precise thing the first says cannot be done. They may be meant for different records, with the flags belonging only to decisions and the silent part approval to requests and plans, but neither record says so, and CA-015 states that a decision's approval is the same enacted full, part and held that CA-013 describes.
