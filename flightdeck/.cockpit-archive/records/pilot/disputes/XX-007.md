# XX-007 — The constitution's rooms are not the cockpit's rooms

One side is the record CA-042, extracted from `cockpit.constitution` lines 113 to 115. The other is the cockpit's rooms as they stand on disk.

## CA-042

"Cockpit locations from the top level; base/, records/, logs/, manuals/, teams/, work/.
Important secondary places; records -> /commander, /pilot, pilot/. logs -> shift/, notepad/, extract/. manuals -> operators/, technical/. teams -> dispatch/, crew/, officers/, rosters/. work -> missions/, workshop." [113-114]

## The cockpit's rooms on disk

`base/`, `commander/`, `records/`, `team/`, `work/` at the cockpit root, alongside `CLAUDE.md` and `cockpit.constitution`. `records/logs/`, `records/manuals/`, `records/dispatch/`, `records/extracts/` and `records/notepad/` sit inside `records/`, not at the top level. `team/crew/`, `team/officers/` and `team/rosters/` sit inside the singular `team/`, not a plural `teams/`.

## The contradiction

CA-042 names six rooms at the top level and the cockpit has five plus two root files. `logs/` and `manuals/` are top level rooms in CA-042 and are `records/logs/` and `records/manuals/` on disk; CA-042's `teams/` is `team/` on disk, singular; CA-042 puts `dispatch/` under teams and the cockpit has it under `records/`; CA-042's `logs -> shift/, notepad/, extract/` are `records/logs/`, `records/notepad/` and `records/extracts/`, three siblings rather than a room with three parts. Every agent that reads CA-042 will look for paths that are not there, and every agent that reads the cockpit's rooms on disk will write to paths CA-042 does not describe. One side has to move: either CA-042 is amended to the cockpit as built, or a mission moves the rooms to match CA-042. One further slip is noted here rather than disputed separately, since it reads as a typing error: CA-042 says "records -> /commander, /pilot, pilot/", naming the pilot's room twice, the second time without a leading slash.
