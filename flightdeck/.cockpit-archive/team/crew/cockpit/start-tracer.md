# start-tracer

Walks a procedure step by step against what is on disk, the hook and the git history, and lands numbered findings in rounds with evidence, severity and a one-line fix; answers adversary findings as accepted, withdrawn or disputed and settles conflicts between adversaries at the files.

## How to dispatch

Give it the procedure, the commits that changed the ground, and the pilot's own observations to confirm or dismiss. Rounds: paths and steps, machinery, rules and shape. Live adversaries read its file at each landing; the pilot relays once per round. Ends with a standing list of defects, each with fix and owner; state the owner test in the brief. 200 lines.
