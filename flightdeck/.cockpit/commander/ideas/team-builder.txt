Maintenance

  - harvests data from logs, dispatches, pilot records and commander outputs, if required.

Axis Understanding:
  - Speed: how quickly can the team run finish and its token efficiency but keep performance?
  - Coverage: How wide does the team cast a net for source material?
  - Quality: the teams' result has project fit and small footprint?
  - Alignment: team aligns with external and internal best practices?(anthropic, manuals)
  - Security: does the team cross boundaries and look where it shouldn't?
  - Adversarial: can the team justify its output against criticism?
