## DS001

### what a launch is

I recomend deeper research into session transcripts. a launch and a run was defined as part of the core changes discussion. A launch is definitely not "one run of one spec". I have defined it better in conversation, but it is an intent to complete a piece of work and includes all the infrastructure to do this. This includes "runs" which are attempts at completing the work, the spec and its versions as runs converge on done and alot of run generated artifacts etc.

### the doctrine

The doctrine I think contains more than found. It has a "source" of a bunch of local web pages. The system is slighty changing to my tastes but is primarily based on this inspiration.

The orchestration principles where derived from those sources with a bit of "fitment" they are very important documents that the original all in one builder tried to replicate the system from. Systems like flightcrew grow organicly but these types of genesis documents are important to update and maintain over time, especially for a pilot who needs to understand where things are at.

The intention for you is not to replicate library documents but rather extract key findings and "record" them as research backed records, to guide the pilot in their tasks. In summary: flighdeck/library = global documentation for all stakeholders and interested parties. .cockpit/records = the pilots most important and trusted records to enable them to do their job specifically and in isolation.

The library should be maintained by you, although other parties may write and read to/from it. This is the distinction, library is for all, records are yours. You have more overview of the system than any other entity.
