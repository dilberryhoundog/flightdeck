## Commander Advice.

Mining the commanders' statements is very useful for fine tuning documents and updating them due to the system drifting. however writing them before their usfulness can be determined purely from the commanders mined statements, is causing the commander to waste time filtering through them for usefuleness and relevance. 

My suggestion is to build them "on demand". In moments where the pilot drifts or underperforms in their role, write a record to address the knowledge gap. this can allow better fine tuning. A wider net can be cast with source, library, manual, commander directives if we have a narrower focus and the content will be current. Some of this content. 

I would rather see the "locally" sourced documents written in plain register rather than blocks of the commanders quick and dirty conversational statements. I suggest the validation of these documents be team-based rather than "source in the prose"  style. What I mean by "team based validation" is if we have a specialized team check the prose against the sources (commander transcripts, library and manuals, source html). Then we can "stamp" them as valid. I suspect the requirment to be validated is encouraging the "source in the prose" for local documents, lowering the quality of the prose.

These records are light and of limited usefulness, because they only call upon commanders' transcripts, often the commander is quoting a portion of a larger previously described principle or instruction set.  

The commanders vision for the system is under serious construction right now and i can see becoming subject to large changes in the near future. My sense is that pouring time into record keeping may be better spent elsewhere.
