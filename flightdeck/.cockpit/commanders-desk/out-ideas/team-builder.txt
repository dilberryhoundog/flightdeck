Introduce a team builder team.
Setup
  - Questionaires team purpose and solution sought. Asks for any relevant context.
  - Offers options for optimal team setup. New or existing.
  - Audits existing seats for suitablity, suggests new seats to complete roster.
  - Ensures Axis: speed, coverage, quality, alignment, security, adversary.
  - Selects type: Autonomous, Commander in the room, Pilot leader.
  - Writes the team roster to meet axis and type requirements. Ensure repeatability.
  - Writes the pilot entry point requirements
  - Writes crew member dossier for team fit.
  - Sets up team improvement criteria and testing methods.
Maintenance
  - Checks for rosters or crew that could be improved to cover team needs.
  - Suggests merged rosters or crew members for better axis alignment.
  - Runs improvement tests and harvests data from sessions, dispatches, pilot records and commander outputs.
  - Prunes or retires rosters and crew members that have been superceeded.

Exploration topics:
  - What axis does a highly effective team require?
  - What team types would cockpit call upon?
  - Are current roster and crew shapes optimal?
