## The split, in the commander's words (later the same session)

Initially it was all on the buildout branch. The commander was trying to fix the core, but with no effective test suite to protect the files and logic that were to be kept. That forced a split: buildout kept as the original build-out; core awaiting the test suite before work could commence there. That is where things are right now: the test suite (characterization) is finished but not merged. The commander spawned the cockpit because they were using an agent role (spec-builder) to manage massive organisational overhead; the pilot exists not to build but to know the system, move and organise work along it, and report to and from the commander.
