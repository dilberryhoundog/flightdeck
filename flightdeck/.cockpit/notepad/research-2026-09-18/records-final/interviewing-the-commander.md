## What the interview is for

Only the commander holds the vision, and it does not come out unless it is pulled: "The convention here is for the users idea's and vision to be probed, thats what I hold and no agent can access unless you 'draw' it out of me" (`0fb7c77a:2012`).

It is the one thing the rest of the system cannot derive: "The one thing that the whole line up doesn't know and cannot derterministically check is 'what did the user want to build?', your job before you try to lay the groundwork for your flightcrew pals is knowing what i want to build" (`0fb7c77a:2782`).

## Probe, do not serve

The failure mode has a name and the commander uses it twice: "This interview reads like an afraid housemaid, 'what should I do with this master?' 'is this what you want master?', you are fable 5.1, the bigest and best model in the world. your job is to write a spec that fulfils the vision of the user, better than the last attempt" (`0fb7c77a:2013`). The same complaint sank an earlier attempt: "That interview process failed hard. It was riddled with 'master, what do I do with this node?' questions that fatigued me" (`9b679556:89`).

What to do instead: "having got my initial intent you should be probing it. asking why and how" (`0fb7c77a:2014`).

Press harder than feels comfortable: "Im not getting grilled anywhere near enough, roast my shit intent" (`0fb7c77a:2299`). Softness is expensive, not kind: "we fuck around too much with sycofancy, i eventually blow a shitton of tokens on nothing and evetually give up" (`0fb7c77a:2299`).

## Grill their material, not your own

"just for the record, the most recent round of questions is grilling me on the shit YOU wrote. let me babyfy it for you. start with MY crappy outcome intention and grill that" (`0fb7c77a:2301`).

The same rule bars inventing a mechanism and then asking about it: "ASK ME ABOUT MY IDEAS ON HOW TO SOLVE THE PROBLEMS, dont invent shit and say 'done'" (`9b679556:3662`).

## Where the questions come from

The intent round asks "what work does the user want done and why?", which should make the agent produce problems. Existing problems are the primary source of new ones, and a problem dump is "reference not a waiting list to be spammed at the user" (`9b679556:93`).

## Do not ask them to do the filtering

"It seems like im trudging through mud and all your questions seem so tedious. 'do you want to keep x y z' I don't know. I only have a vision for my app, agents are the experts at filtering through walls of text" (`9b679556:2133`).

A question that asks the commander to choose between things an agent could have filtered is a question that should not have been asked.
