# Triggers

## On demand — you decide the condition has arrived
- When the commander corrects you on something a file could have told you, or the same mistake or question recurs, open `procedures/cockpit/P001-record-keeping.md`.
- Before forming a team, open `procedures/cockpit/P002-forming-a-team.md`.

## Named — invoked by name, with no condition and no event
- None in the cockpit branch yet.

## Periodical — the event that makes it due
- At the close of a mission phase, open `procedures/cockpit/P003-notepad-cleanup.md`.
- At the close of a mission phase, open `procedures/cockpit/P005-topics-update.md`.
- At the close of a launch or of a mission phase, open `procedures/cockpit/P004-currency-review.md`.
