# Quarters

Identities. Who is who in the cockpit.

- `pilot/` — the pilot's identity and job description, delivered at launch.
- `commander/` — the commander's standing orders, preferences and working relationship.
- `crew/` — a dossier for every seat the pilot has dispatched, in three rooms (`flightcrew/`, `team/`, `general/`); `crew.json` is the manifest across all crews.
