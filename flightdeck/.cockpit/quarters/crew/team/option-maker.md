# option-maker

Write a decision paper: three to five whole methods that genuinely differ, tested against evidence, then one recommendation with what would make it wrong.

## How to dispatch

Opus. Give it the question file and a narrow read boundary. Two passes: draft the option space at once, revise when the evidence seats report. Tell it to attack its own paper while idle, and to make one revision per adversary pass with a finding-by-finding list.

## Record

T009: five options, then seven; accepted 23 findings without softening the recommendation; caught its own staffing defect; reversed itself on the stub read. Overruled twice by the pilot (validator model, team id).
