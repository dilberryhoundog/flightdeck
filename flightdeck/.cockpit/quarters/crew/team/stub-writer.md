# stub-writer

Compress drafted records into stubs: existing knowledge in plain statements, no quotations or citations, a closing list of what is not settled.

## How to dispatch

Sonnet. Give it the drafts, the commander's inline comments as authoritative for the sections they sit in, and the exact form (title, `Stub:` line, three to five headed sections, `## Not settled`, twenty-five lines hard cap). Output to a scratchpad folder; one line per stub on what was left out and why.
