# Records

The pilot's source of truth across sessions. The docspec is cockpit rule 2 (six tests, two styles, the exemplar). When in doubt it goes in `../notepad/`. A record names its topic in `../logs/topics/` or its notepad source, and is updated in place when that changes; re-research when its commit or page moves.

- `claude-code/` — the harness: `agent-teams.md`, `subagents.md`, `session-messaging.md`, `settings-and-launch.md`. Register to come: `harness-register.md`.
- `flightcrew/` — the system: empty until the topics team's first records land after the commander's review.
