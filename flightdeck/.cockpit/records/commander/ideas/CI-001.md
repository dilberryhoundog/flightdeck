# CI-001 — A test harness and evaluation suite for the cockpit

## The commander's words

"test harness and fixtures with an evaluation suite for cockpit functionality and behaviours. Challenging over an agent based project, claude plugin eval is a native eval suite for testing improvements to agent bodies. Rails 8 also has a native evaluation suite for improving agents."

## What it says

The commander wants a way to test that the cockpit works and behaves as it should: a harness, fixtures to run against, and an evaluation suite over both the cockpit's functions and its behaviours. He notes in the same breath that this is hard on a project made of agents, and names two suites that already exist and might serve: `claude plugin eval`, which is native to Claude Code and made for testing changes to agent bodies, and the evaluation suite native to Rails 8.

## Why it is held as an idea and not as advice

Nothing here is settled. The first sentence names a want without a subject or a verb, the second calls the problem challenging, and the third offers a second option beside the first without choosing between them. There is no location, no shape and no decision about which suite the cockpit uses, and the constitution's own description of an idea at line 36 fits it: a written exploration, filled with records that are still maturing, held in draft and inert while it forms.

## Where it sits

The whole of the Testing section of `cockpit.constitution`, line 120, which is two paragraphs long. The other paragraph, at line 122, does state a requirement — that crew and teams receive improvement strategies and that those strategies are maintained — and it is filed as advice at `../advice/crew/CA-045.json`, under crew rather than testing because that is the infrastructure it shapes.

## What would make it live

A commander record invoking it, as line 36 requires. On the constitution's own reading it would then generally become a mission, since choosing a suite, building the fixtures and getting the cockpit under test is work that runs over time and over many dispatches rather than a single change.
