# Notepad

Scratch space. Nothing here is authoritative and anything may be deleted. Notepad entries are the pathway to records. When entries accumulate around a subject, they guide a research task, and the researched result is promoted into `records/` under the rules in `records/README.md`. A note that matters but will never be a record goes into `logs/` or a mission file. The commander also permits scratch in the `.claude` folder, a side room to the cockpit.

- `claude-code/launch-tests.md` — our `claude -p` launch tests (T1 to T12): settings, hooks, persona, guard, model and effort, append-prompt delivery, resume by name.
- `claude-code/messaging-observed.md` — observations of live ListAgents and SendMessage traffic.
- `claude-code/pilot-choice.md` — the pilot's choice of instruments and the launcher implication.
- `flightcrew/` — first survey of the flightcrew system (2026-09-17), digest and opinion, awaiting relevance.
- `team-run-2026-09-18/` — first agent team: auditor and docs-scout findings, red-team verdicts, observations of team mechanics.
- `team/` — the pilot's working practices for staffing and dispatching crew.
- `claude-md-review-2026-09-18/` — second team run: docs-scout (official CLAUDE.md docs, F1 to F21), cockpit-comber (first pass) and claude-md-judge (verdicts applied to CLAUDE.md on 2026-09-18).
- `decision-2026-09-19/` — decision team T009 on record keeping: the question, auditor and scout reports, the option-maker's paper (v1 and final), two adversary passes, the `@` import live test. Distilled as DS004.
