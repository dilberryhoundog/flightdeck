T014, mission M001, 2026-09-20, seat `commander-advocate`, dispatched by session `pilot`. Sources read: `notepad/refit-2026-09-20/mini-spec.md`; orders O044 to O057 (`text` only); the seven idea files in `commanders-desk/out-ideas/`; the worked frontmatter in DS001, P004 and CA001; D026 and D027; `notepad/alignment-2026-09-20/advised.md` and `alignment.md`; `base/store/prefixes.json` to check claims.

The spec is much closer to his words than DS006 was. Most of Scope in is quotable to him. What follows is what is not.

## Drifts, ranked

**1. Stage A is removed; he said keep it.** His [O057]: "Use any temporary fixtures to help refit the cockpit... Stage A is kept around as a help, out of the way." Spec, Constraints: "The stage A tools in `base/bin/` and `base/store/` are temporary help only and are removed at the end." This is the one place the spec contradicts an order in plain words, and it is a deletion, which is the hardest thing to take back. Cure: keep the constraint's first half, replace "removed at the end" with "moved out of the way at the end, location the pilot's choice"; put deletion to him separately.

**2. The idea files he ordered are missing from the spec entirely.** His [O057]: "then create adjacent idea files from the commander's initial ideas expanded with his later conversational confirmations, named out-ideas/<ace>_<idea>; the commander will remix them and possibly start again with the improved build flow." The spec has no such item in Scope in and does not exclude it in Scope out. His own next move depends on these files ("possibly start again"), so to him they may matter more than the build. Cure: add a seventh step, or state that it is a separate dispatch and when.

**3. "Based upon existing data shapes" has been turned into its opposite.** His [O057]: "Schemas for all document types, based on existing data shapes." Spec: "all based on the shapes documents already have" (the word kept) — but step 4 rewrites every document's frontmatter, and the first verification is "`cockpit-lint` fails the commander's DS001 example on its comma lists". A schema built from existing shapes cannot fail the existing shape. The schemas describe the target, not the corpus, and the spec does not say so. Cure: say it plainly — "schemas describe the conventions documents will follow after step 4; today's corpus is brought to them" — and let him rule.

**4. Flightcrew's existing schema assets are not mentioned.** His [O046]: "Flightcrew has these schema checking assets already built, on other branches; the spec builder was using a linter recently, along with rubric checkers." Spec: a new linter, "one file, standard-library Python 3.9, no dependencies, a few hundred lines at most", with no line on looking at what exists. `alignment.md` line 12 lists this as the exact omission last time ("not delivered, and explicitly excluded"). Cure: one line — either "reuses flightcrew's `schema-lib.mjs`" or "checked and not reused, because <reason>". Do not leave it silent twice.

**5. The frontmatter shape rejects his worked examples.** His [frontmatter.txt]: "A few legacy documents already have examples. to demonstrate the shape." Those examples read `stamp: "2026-09-18", "Pilot: Ace", "session dcb75456"` and `generates: "P004", "P005"` (DS001), and CA001 carries two fields only, `type` and `unit`. Spec: "strict flat YAML (bare value, quoted string, bracketed list)", `stamp: [date, author, session]`, plus `stamp`, `work`, `context`, `generates` on "every ID-based work and desk document" — which is CA001. Bracketed lists are the pilot's choice, not his; so is growing the CA shape from two fields to six. Cure: name bracketing as the pilot's convention under R4 ("Pilot decides convention"), and ask him whether desk out-advice really carries the full set.

**6. `context` as ids-only drops what his example holds.** His [O051]: "'context' is the contextual position this document maintains: work is the umbrella, generates is follow-up documents; it is a chain, for example the context for a Ds should be a team dispatch." Spec: "`context: [...]` ... as ids forming the chain". DS001's context today is prose naming the recon folder and how it was verified. His sentence licenses an id for a Ds; it does not say prose is banned everywhere, and the verification "every `context` and `generates` id resolves" makes prose a lint failure. Cure: allow one trailing free string, or state the loss.

**7. The flat dispatch folder is settled where he left it open and where his own sketch says otherwise.** One register is his [O051, D027]. The folder is not: [O052] "a global manifest (MANIFEST-dispatches), with team rooms dispatch/flightcrew/T003... but it could easily be a flat dispatch folder. Early on flat seems better, but after three months and up to T364, dispatch rooms may be more readable", and `cockpit-setup.txt` draws rooms with `MANIFEST-dispatch-cockpit.json` per room. Spec: "One dispatch register for the whole team space, the room a field, units in one flat folder." "The room a field" is the pilot's invention and a good one. Cure: put it to him in one sentence (gap 1 below); do not build it as decided.

**8. The crew room he drew is renamed.** His [cockpit-setup.txt]: `crew/` holds `flightcrew/`, `general/`, `cockpit/`. **9. ## Does the verification show him the right thing was built

Partly. It proves the rename ran, the refs resolve and nothing forbidden was touched — mechanical facts. It does not test his purpose. Missing: (a) nothing checks that a schema exists for *every* document type ([O057] "Schemas for all document types"), and a lint that passes "every typed document" passes trivially if few types are typed — count the types and the schemas and print both; (b) nothing tests [O056], that a new session writing from the schema alone produces a document that passes — hand the schema to a fresh cheap agent and lint what it writes; (c) nothing measures "saves the most tokens and reduces churn" — print the clean-run byte count and the full-corpus run, as `alignment.md` line 14 did; (d) nothing checks the lint is non-blocking (exit status 0 on findings); (e) step 2's "a case-sensitive search finds no old-form id" will hit `orders.json` text, session logs and `logs/crew-manifest.json`, which Constraints forbid changing, so the check must name its exclusions or it will be fudged on the day. The closing commander-advocate pass is the strongest item in the list and should stay.
