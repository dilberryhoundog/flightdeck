## 1. His six axis definitions against topic 1's

- **Speed** — "how quickly can the team run finish and its token efficiency but keep performance?" The paper's time half survives: relay rounds to settle, with parallel width and the longest dependency chain as the structural predictors. **Token efficiency moves inside Speed**, and "but keep performance" makes it a ratio rather than a minimum, so Speed cannot be scored alone — a team that halves its tokens and loses a topic scores worse, not better. Rounds of rework, which the paper filed under cost, are time and belong here too.
- **Coverage** — "How wide does the team cast a net for source material?" **Replaced.** The paper scored the share of the question's parts with a seat accountable; his is source breadth. Scored instead as the distinct source classes reached: official pages fetched raw, orders and decisions, logs, dispatch units, notepad reports, transcripts, schemas and the filesystem. This flight scores high on his definition — the scout refetched every cited page raw, the miner read nine logs plus orders, decisions, advice and seventeen dispatch units, the auditor enumerated the schemas and the tree. The pre-registered parts list survives, but as a completeness device for the answer, not as the coverage measure.
- **Quality** — "the teams' result has project fit and small footprint?" **Replaced, and it cuts against this paper.** Footprint is measurable as lines added, files touched and new fields or schemas introduced. Topic 3 proposes six new roster and dispatch fields plus four dossier keys; on his Quality axis that is a cost, not a gain, and the paper never weighed it as one. The checker argument survives better than the fields argument, since a checker adds no fields.
- **Alignment** — "team aligns with external and internal best practices?(anthropic, manuals)" **Replaced entirely.** The paper's Alignment was conformance to his intent, scored from rulings dated after a team landed; his is conformance to practice. So the paper's "Alignment is the largest failure bucket, 18 items" was measuring something he does not call Alignment. On his definition the serving seats are `practice-scout`, `docs-verifier` and `validator`, and the measure is claims checked against a cited source before use. Intent-conformance does not disappear — it is the pilot's job and `commander-advocate`'s — but it is not this axis and should not be scored as one.
- **Security** — "does the team cross boundaries and look where it shouldn't?" **Survives and shifts weight to the read side**, which the paper had but underweighted in favour of write scope. It also becomes the one axis with two measured events this flight: both pooled adversaries hit out-of-scope file content through a grep and each disclosed it unprompted rather than suppressing it. That is the axis working, and self-disclosure is the thing to reward in a brief.
- **Adversarial** — "can the team justify its output against criticism?" **Replaced, from a process test to an outcome test.** The paper scored whether review happened and how findings were taken; his asks whether the output stands. This flight's score on his definition is legible and high: 42 cold findings from two seats reading blind, none of which unseated a topic or a recommendation. It also retires the withdrawal ratio the paper was already struggling with — under his definition a withdrawal is neither credit nor debit, only the surviving output counts.

## 2. The axes as tuning dimensions, per type, with this flight's events

His framing: "not really an axis-to-seat question. rather the teams performance tuned across axis. what tradeoffs or inclusions the team need to make to perform on these axises?" So each axis is a dial, and a type sets where the dial can sit.

- **Autonomous** — buys Speed, pays Adversarial and Security. No ruling between rounds means nothing catches a drift, and the permission budget (twenty blocks, a stall at three in a row) is the hard stop. Inclusion that moves Adversarial without a human: a cold adversary dispatched at the end, which Or081 makes possible with the same body. Never observed in T001 to T017, so this row is predicted.
- **Pilot leader** — tunes Alignment-to-practice up cheaply, because the pilot can hold a verifier seat in scope, and tunes Speed down through relay. Its measured tradeoff this flight: the lead's relay of rounds 2 and 3 after a write began cost a whole pass, which is Speed paid for nothing.
- **Commander in the room** — maximum intent-conformance, lowest Speed; T010 took eleven passes and this flight eight. The inclusion that protects Speed is the numbered freeze, and the events prove it both ways: freezes held for passes 4 to 8, and the one crossing, at 15:22:38 against 15:23:08, cost a re-read.
- **Commander at the seat** — trades a relay round for a record gap, since a ruling given at a seat never reaches `commander/orders/`. The inclusion that pays for it is the filing step.
- **This flight's team** — Coverage was tuned high by three parallel evidence seats on disjoint sources; Adversarial by four attackers in two postures, live and cold; Speed was the dial left loose, at eight passes, and the token cost was never measured, which his Speed definition now requires. Quality by his footprint measure is untested: the paper proposes ten new keys and one checker.
