bundle P-constraints · constraints

Constraints are the conditions that were true before this work and outlive it. Six are read straight from the manuals and the harness. Two are yours: what a live trial may cost, and what a live probe may never do.

Q1 C5

Is there a ceiling on what the run may spend on live trials, and if so in what unit?

Statistical and judged checks run real sessions. The addendum says keep scenario sets small; a number turns that into a check the run can be held to.

Q2 C8

A live probe exercises the real system: it can open worktrees, cut branches, run hooks and commands. What must it never do?

C8 as drafted forbids reaching a remote. Anything else forbidden has to be written, because a probe that can do a thing will eventually do it.
