flightcrew inventory · keep / goes / unsure

Every current part of the system, grouped by folder. Tick each one. Keep means the characterization suite covers it; goes means the rebuild removes or replaces it and the suite ignores it; unsure becomes a question. A comment on a keep can say what about it is kept (its behaviour, its shape, its bytes).
