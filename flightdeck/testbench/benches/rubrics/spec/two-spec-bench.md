## What the rubric did and did not carry over

- Carried over cleanly: QIFC.1's shape test, QBEH.2's check-based test, QDOD.2's resolution step, QGEN.1's provenance exemption. None of these produced disagreement on the new material.
- New ground the interviewer spec never exercised: QPRI.1 (requirements in decision form), QBEH.1 (unnameable results), QVER.3 on an undeclared agent-shaped product, QCON.1 on rationale clauses. These are where fable and opus split on the new specs, and they split the same way as before: fable literal, opus charitable.
- Two candidate v2.4 lines from this bench: QVER.3 applies when the intent names an agent, session or role as the product, declared or not; QPRI.1 a decision that states a rule on the product's conduct is a requirement unless a constraint or behaviour carries the same rule.

## Absorber residue

On the orchestrator, every finding in both chains' final runs was on text the absorber wrote while splitting: B9's rewrite (two clauses), B12's rewrite (two properties), B24's In field, "report-ready" in ACC. A tuning chain measures the rubric and the absorber together; the harness should score absorber-introduced findings separately (finding on original text vs finding on absorbed text) so the rubric's convergence is not charged with the absorber's.
